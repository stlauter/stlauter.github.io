The Author

I've found that poetry lacks true romance, when done for the articulation
of experience, rather than for the expression of misunderstanding. And while I
may be a hypocrite to my own beliefs, often writing about my life and memories,
I truly shine when it comes to conveying, even if vaugely, emotions that
cannot be voiced otherwise. This being what I believe to be the true purpose of poetry.

Growing up with autism I struggled to clearly communicate how and what I was
feeling. This feeling of misunderstanding didn't go away as I got older, and it
led me to pursue english. For with a larger lexicon, surely I could get a point
across. And it worked. So well in fact that I found comfort in embellishing my words.
Articulating excruciating feelings in lovely ways. And creating art out of despair.
I started writing at 12 years old and have been writing almost daily since. Creating
poems whenever I hear a certain song, or see, feel, or smell a certain thing.

I'm an aspiring poet, and once I have enough poems digitalized I plan to publish a book
of my own. So by consequence I could one day become an artist whose words are as immortalized
as much as the dead poets who came before me.
